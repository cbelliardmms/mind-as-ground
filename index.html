Mind as Ground

What if the deepest crisis in modern cosmology — the Hubble tension, the vacuum energy catastrophe, the measurement problem — points not to missing particles but to a missing premise? This is an argument that analytic idealism offers a coherent resolution, and an exploration of what follows for the nature of human and machine consciousness alike.
